✅ CORAL ordinal loss
Already in your code. Perfectly suited for DR 0→4 grading. The biggest single algorithmic change from standard CE.
+0.03–0.04 QWK over CrossEntropy. Keep as-is.
✅ EMA — change decay to 0.9997
Your current decay=0.999 is too aggressive for 60 epochs — shadow weights lag behind the model. At 0.9997 the EMA tracks more slowly and accumulates a better average over the full run.
+0.01 QWK on val. Zero risk.
❌ Mixup — DISABLE
Your code has use_mixup=True. Mixup + CORAL ordinal is mathematically inconsistent: mixing a Grade 0 and Grade 4 image does NOT create a valid Grade 2 eye. The ordinal targets become nonsense. This will silently hurt QWK.
Set use_mixup: False. Removing this gains you 0.01–0.02 QWK.
✅ SWA — keep, tune LR
Already in code from epoch 45. SWA LR should be 1/10 of your cosine annealing final LR. Your current swa_lr: 1e-5 is correct. Keep it exactly as-is.
+0.01 QWK. Already configured correctly.
✅ Gradient accumulation → 4 steps
Your current accum=3 with batch=18 gives effective batch 54. Change to accum=4 with batch=16 → effective batch 64. Batch 64 is the sweet spot for APTOS — better gradient estimates at grade boundaries.
Effective batch 64. Stable, no OOM risk at 384px.
✅ Label smoothing → 0.10
Reduce from 0.12 to 0.10. Your current 0.12 is slightly too high — it over-softens grade 0 (which is the most clear-cut class). 0.10 is the DR inter-rater disagreement literature consensus value.
+0.005 QWK. Tiny but free.
✅ CoarseDropout — keep, it's your best aug
Randomly blanks patches of the fundus, forcing the model to diagnose from partial information — exactly what happens with cataract/media opacity. Fast, fundus-appropriate, and provably helps generalisation.
Keep as-is. One of the best fundus-specific augmentations.
⚠️ image_size: 384 not 512
At 512px: DataParallel scatters 8 images per GPU. With AMP activations for B4-NS that's ~13.5 GB peak — dangerously close to T4's 15 GB limit. A single spike = OOM crash. 384px peak is ~7.5 GB — fully safe.
512 risks OOM. 384 is 95% of the accuracy at 50% the risk.
